# 🐄 Dairy Farm Management System

A web-based **Dairy Farm Management System** developed to manage and automate the day-to-day activities of a dairy farm.

The system provides separate functionality for managing farm information, users, workers, customers, suppliers, products, milk collection, orders, payments, feedback, and other farm-related operations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Dairy Farm Management System** is designed to reduce manual work and provide a centralized system for managing dairy farm operations.

The system allows administrators and workers to manage farm activities through a web-based interface, while customers can place orders for available dairy products.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Manage dairy farm information efficiently.
* Manage users, workers, customers, and suppliers.
* Maintain dairy product information and stock.
* Record milk collection details.
* Manage customer orders.
* Manage order items and payments.
* Collect customer feedback.
* Reduce manual record keeping.
* Provide a centralized database for farm information.
* Improve accuracy and efficiency of dairy farm operations.
